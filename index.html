Estimate only, not tax advice. Uses 2026 federal brackets, the $184,500 Social Security wage base, and 2026 state individual income tax brackets & standard deductions (Source: Tax Foundation, "2026 State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets"). State tax is computed on the W-2 salary less the state standard deduction. A few state-specific rules are NOT modeled: local/county income taxes; CA's 1%+ mental-health surtax and SDI payroll tax; CT and NY "tax benefit recapture"; phaseouts of deductions/exemptions at high incomes; and personal/dependent exemptions and credits. WA, TN, NH, FL, TX, NV, SD, AK, WY are treated as no wage-income-tax states. The equivalence solves for the salary at which the employee's net take-home plus employer benefits matches their current 1099 net. Run real numbers past a CPA before making an offer.
